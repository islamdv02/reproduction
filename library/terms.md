## List

- **CPG (Central Pattern Generator)**: Autonomous neural networks within the spinal cord and brainstem capable of producing rhythmic and coordinated motor activity patterns (e.g., locomotion, respiration) in the absence of rhythmic sensory inputs or descending timing commands. [Marder & Bucher, 2001; PubMed: [11728329](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11728329/), Google Scholar: [Link](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Central+pattern+generators+and+the+control+of+rhythmic+movements+Marder+Bucher)]
- **CST (Corticospinal Tract)**: The major descending motor pathway in mammals originating from cerebral cortical motor areas and projecting directly or via spinal interneurons to motor neurons, critical for the voluntary execution of fine, fractionated distal movements. [Lemon, 2008; PubMed: [18558853](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18558853/), Google Scholar: [Link](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=Descending+pathways+in+motor+control+Lemon)]
- **RNA (Ribonucleic Acid)**: A single-stranded biological polymer composed of ribonucleotide units that plays fundamental cellular and neural roles in genetic coding, protein translation, enzymatic catalysis, and gene regulation across mRNA, tRNA, rRNA, and non-coding RNA species. [Sharp, 2009; PubMed: [19239877](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19239877/), Google Scholar: [Link](https://scholar.google.com/scholar?q=The+centrality+of+RNA+Sharp)]
